# Marko Ranković

**Software Engineer · Full Stack · Architecture & Technical Leadership**

10+ years building software end to end, from the database to the browser. I currently work on conversational AI, building products on top of LLMs and AI agents.

I work where product, architecture, and delivery meet: I take an ambiguous problem, shape it into a plan that several teams can execute, and carry it through to production.

## What I do

- **Set technical direction.** I write the design proposals and RFCs that turn product goals into architecture. I make the tradeoffs explicit so the decisions still make sense a year later.
- **Lead work across teams.** I break vague or cross-cutting problems into scoped, sequenced work and coordinate it across teams and services until it ships.
- **Own systems end to end.** I design, build, and run services across the stack, and I weigh reliability and maintainability as heavily as features.
- **Improve how the team builds.** I introduce the tooling, conventions, and AI-assisted workflows that make the whole team faster, and I replace tribal knowledge with guardrails.
- **Align stakeholders.** I agree on scope, quality attributes, and what "done" means with product, design, and leadership before anyone writes code.

## How I work

- **Simple over clever.** The best design is the one the next engineer understands without asking me.
- **Fix the root cause.** A patch that hides a symptom is a bug for later.
- **Prove it works.** Verify on the running system, not on "it compiles".
- **Write it down.** Decisions, alternatives, and rationale live in documents, not in someone's head.
